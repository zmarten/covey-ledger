# Covey launch copy

Use these drafts after the landing page is deployed and the waitlist is verified. Public posts under Zach's identity should be reviewed before publishing.

## One-sentence pitch

Covey Ledger is a field-to-freezer hunting log for upland hunters who want Garmin-friendly field capture, clean bird splits, freezer inventory, and possession-limit context in one place.

## Short positioning

Most hunting apps focus on maps, waypoints, or scouting. Covey Ledger focuses on what happens after the flush: what was shot, who it belongs to, what is in the freezer, and whether the next hunt is still comfortably inside daily and possession limits.

## Landing-page headline variants

1. The hunting logbook that follows birds from the field to the freezer.
2. Turn Garmin hunt notes into a practical freezer and possession ledger.
3. Track upland hunts, bird splits, and freezer inventory without another messy spreadsheet.

5. Know what happened in the field — and what is still in the freezer.

## Reddit / forum draft

Title: Looking for feedback: field-to-freezer log for upland hunters

I am validating an early idea for upland hunters who track hunts with Garmin/onX/paper notes and then lose the thread once birds get split, gifted, frozen, or eaten.

The workflow I am testing is:

- quick field/session capture
- bird splits across a hunting party
- freezer inventory by species/state
- simple daily + possession-limit context
- exportable records for responsible hunting

This is not meant to replace mapping/scouting tools. It is more of a field-to-freezer ledger.

If that sounds useful, confusing, or unnecessary, I would appreciate blunt feedback. Landing page/waitlist: https://covey.zachmartens.com/

Questions I am trying to answer:

1. Do you track harvested birds after the hunt?
2. Where does your current process break down?
3. Would Garmin/watch capture matter, or is phone/paper enough?
4. Is possession/freezer tracking useful or overkill?
